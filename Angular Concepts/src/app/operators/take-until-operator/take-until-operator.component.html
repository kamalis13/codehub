Syntax — Angular ngOnDestroy pattern

  

  
takeUntil()

  
Emits values from the source Observable until a second "notifier" Observable emits. This is the standard Angular pattern for automatically unsubscribing in ngOnDestroy to prevent memory leaks.

  
Analogy: Like a subscription that auto-cancels when you close your account — you just signal "done" once and everything cleans up.
