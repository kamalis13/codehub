Syntax

  

  
withLatestFrom()

  
When the source Observable emits, it combines the emission with the MOST RECENT value from another Observable. The other Observable is sampled — it doesn't trigger anything, it's just "read" at the moment the source emits.

  
Analogy: Like checking the scoreboard when the buzzer sounds — the buzzer (source) triggers the read, and you grab whatever the scoreboard shows at that moment.
