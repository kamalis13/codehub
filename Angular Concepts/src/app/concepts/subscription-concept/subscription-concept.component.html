Subscription

"The live connection between an Observable and an Observer — cancel it to stop the stream."

unsubscribe()

Connection cut

📖
Definition

A Subscription is the object returned by subscribe(). It represents the active connection to an Observable stream. Calling unsubscribe() cancels it and frees memory.

📺
Analogy

A Netflix subscription — you subscribe to start watching. If you don't cancel, you keep getting charged (memory leak in code!). Cancel it to stop.

⚠️
Why it matters

Long-lived Observables (timers, WebSockets, route params) never stop on their own. If you don't unsubscribe when a component is destroyed, the callback keeps running in the background — this is a memory leak.
