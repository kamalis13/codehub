🔢 Concept 5

Observer

"The listener — reacts to every value, error, and completion from an Observable."

📡
Observable PRODUCER

Emits values over time

next(value) error(err) complete()
→
📻
Observer CONSUMER

Reacts to each event

📦
next(value)

Called every time the Observable emits a new value. This is where your main logic goes — updating UI, storing data, etc.

❌
error(err)

Called if something goes wrong. After an error, the stream STOPS — no more next() or complete() will fire.

✅
complete()

Called once when the Observable finishes successfully. Use for cleanup — hide spinners, etc.
