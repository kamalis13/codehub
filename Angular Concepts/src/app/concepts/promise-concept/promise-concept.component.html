🔢 Concept 3

Promise

"I promise to give you ONE result later — either success or failure."

🟡
PENDING

Working… not done yet

→
✅
FULFILLED

Success! Value arrived

or
❌
REJECTED

Failed with an error

📖
Definition

A Promise handles ONE future async value. It's either pending, fulfilled (value arrived), or rejected (error). Once settled it never changes state.

📦
Real-life analogy

An online order tracking number. You don't have the package yet, but you're promised it. It'll either arrive (fulfilled) or be lost (rejected).
