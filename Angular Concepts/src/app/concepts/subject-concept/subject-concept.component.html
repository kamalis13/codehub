📻
Subject

A live broadcast — everyone subscribed hears it at once

📖
Definition

A Subject is simultaneously an Observable (you can subscribe to it) and an Observer (you can push values into it). It multicasts — all subscribers receive the same value at the same time.

📻
Analogy

A live radio broadcast. The station (Subject) sends a signal. Everyone who has tuned in receives it. Tune in late? You missed it — Subject has no memory of past broadcasts.

⚠️
No replay

Values emitted before a subscriber joins are lost to that late subscriber. Use BehaviorSubject or ReplaySubject if you need late subscribers to get past values.
