📖
Definition

An Observable is a blueprint for a stream. It does nothing until subscribed to. Once subscribed, it can emit any number of values over time, then complete or error.

📺
Analogy

A YouTube channel — it produces content over time. You subscribe to receive it, and can unsubscribe whenever you want to stop.

😴
Lazy by nature

An Observable is like a recipe — writing the recipe doesn't cook the food. Only when you subscribe() does it start executing.
