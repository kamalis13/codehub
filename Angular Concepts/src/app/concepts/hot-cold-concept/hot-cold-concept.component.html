Core Concept

Hot vs Cold Observables

Two ways a stream can behave when subscribed to

🧊
Cold Observable — creates a new, independent data producer for EACH subscriber. Like a Netflix movie: every viewer starts from the beginning.
🔥
Hot Observable — shares one running producer with ALL subscribers. Like live TV: you join mid-broadcast and miss what already aired.
🧊 Cold Observable
  • Producer created PER subscriber
  • Late subscriber gets full stream
  • Examples: of(), from(), interval(), HTTP calls
🔥 Hot Observable
  • Producer shared across all subscribers
  • Late subscriber misses past values
  • Examples: Subject, DOM events, WebSockets
